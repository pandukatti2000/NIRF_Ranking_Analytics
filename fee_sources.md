# NIRF 2025 Engineering Colleges: Fee Sources, Methodology & Citations

## 1. Target Fee Definition & Methodology

The fee definition standardized across this study is:

> **Total mandatory academic fees for a standard four-year B.Tech/B.E. programme for a newly admitted general-category Indian student, excluding hostel, mess, transport, personal expenses, and refundable deposits.**

### Standardization & Comparability Rules:
1. **Admission Year Preference**: AY 2024–25 / 2025–26 official fee circulars.
2. **Statutory National Frameworks**:
   - **IIT System**: Mandated by IIT Council / Ministry of Education (MHRD F.No.24-2/2016-TS.I) at Rs 1,00,000/sem statutory tuition (Rs 8,00,000 total) + institute non-tuition academic charges and one-time admission dues.
   - **NIT System**: Mandated by Council of NITSER / Ministry of Education at Rs 62,500/sem statutory tuition (Rs 5,00,000 total) + institute-specific annual charges.
   - **JAC Delhi**: Fully determined 4-year statutory graduated schedules for DTU, NSUT, and IIIT-Delhi published in the official Joint Admission Counselling brochure.
   - **State Counselling / Fee Fixation Committees**: TNEA (Tamil Nadu), MHT-CET (Maharashtra), WBJEE (West Bengal), COMEDK/KCET (Karnataka).
3. **Non-Refundable vs Refundable**: Refundable caution money deposits (typically Rs 3,000 to Rs 15,000) and mess/hostel advances are strictly isolated and excluded.
4. **Escalation Accounting**: Institutions with pre-determined annual fee escalations (e.g., BITS Pilani 5% statutory annual escalator, DTU, NSUT) incorporate the compounded 4-year schedule rather than flat first-year multiplication.
